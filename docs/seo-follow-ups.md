# AIS SEO and audience follow-ups

Agreed on 21 September 2026. Priorities: reputation with finance professionals,
newsletter readership, sponsors, student applications, then event attendance.

## Recruitment email list

- Collect an email list of students interested in joining AIS.
- Notify interested students whenever applications open, linking to the current
  application form and deadline.
- Offer newsletter subscription alongside recruitment updates, keeping the two
  preferences clear and independently manageable when the dedicated list is added.
- For now, the About-page FAQ directs prospective members to the existing
  newsletter signup and describes application emails as a planned service.
- The current signup sends requests to the AIS contact inbox through Web3Forms;
  it is not yet a dedicated subscriber database or an automated email service.
- Before launch: choose the email provider, store consent and preferences,
  implement unsubscribe handling, and verify an application-opening email with
  an internal test subscriber. No recurring email campaign has been activated.
